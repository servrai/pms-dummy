Fault injection (polygon controls)

Applies to the deliveries endpoint. Use these to exercise servr's retry / backoff / error-handling semantics:

  • force_500 — transport failure: servr retries with backoff, then parks for staff retry.
  • timeout — hold the connection 65 s (past servr's client timeout).
  • delay — answer normally after N ms.
  • respond_error_code — clean error body with a chosen action_error_code (delivery closes as failed).
